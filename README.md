## Smart Solutions Life Counter For Magic The Gathering
- Built with passion for the player. The Life Counter For Magic The Gathering was built for the love of the game. Magic the Gathering games can become tedious to track as players can have multiple cards in play affecting the other player. Whether it's taking simple damage to a powerful combo, tracking life points accurately can take away some of the fun. The application makes it simple for the players to keep track of their life points, while displaying them easily to each other. Since it is web based it can be taken on the go to play anywhere, from in the home to your local card shop, supported on multiple devices. As a Magic the Gathering player myself, I take pride in making an application that not only I can enjoy but other players as well.
