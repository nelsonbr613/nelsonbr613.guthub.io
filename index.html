Smart Solutions Life Counter for Magic: The Gathering

As a Magic: The Gathering player, I know that keeping track of life points can distract from enjoying the game. Our team is developing a web-based life counter to make this easier.

The current version is planned for two players, each starting with 20 life points. Players will be able to add or subtract life points and reset the totals for a new game. We are developing the website and reviewing its requirements.

I contribute game knowledge, feature ideas, website development, and testing. Our goal is to make the website easy to use on both phones and computers.
